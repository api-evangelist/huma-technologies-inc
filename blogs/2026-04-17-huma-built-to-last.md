HUMA was launched with a simple thesis: the next global payments and liquidity network should be owned by the public. In under a year, Huma’s PayFi network has scaled into core infrastructure powering major remittance flows, now driving ~ $1B in monthly volume, exceeding Visa’s monthly stablecoin volume. This growth was enabled by HUMA, coordinating a broad partner ecosystem, energizing a global community and mobilizing 100,000+ liquidity providers.
